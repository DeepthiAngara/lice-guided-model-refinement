# Dataset source and redistribution

The prepared file `diabetes_brfss2015_prepared.csv` is derived from
`diabetes_binary_5050split_health_indicators_BRFSS2015.csv` in Alex Teboul's
**Diabetes Health Indicators Dataset** on Kaggle:

<https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/alexteboul/diabetes-health-indicators-dataset>

The Kaggle dataset page identifies the dataset license as **CC0: Public
Domain**. CC0 permits copying, redistribution, modification, and preparation of
derivative datasets. The prepared derivative is included here to preserve the
exact retained-row order used by the released train/test and OOF assignments.

The data originate from the 2015 Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System
(BRFSS). The public repository contains no names, contact information, or
direct personal identifiers.
